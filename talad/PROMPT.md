Build a single-file interactive web app (HTML, CSS and JS) using Three.js from a CDN: a live 3D night market operations dashboard called "Talad" that feels like a strategy game.

WORLD: a low-poly isometric Bangkok night market built entirely in code, no external models. Rows of food stalls with striped canopies and string lights, a satay grill with smoke, a mango sticky rice cart, a coconut ice cream stand, a fashion lane with clothes racks, a tuk-tuk rank, a canal with a long-tail boat and a little bridge, a 7-Eleven style corner shop, a skytrain viaduct with a station overhead, a car park, palms and banana trees. Clean toy-like clay look, soft shadows, one accent colour (#FF3D7F hot pink) on warm night navy.

LIFE: shoppers stroll the aisles, queue at stalls, pay, wait and walk off with bags and skewers; vendors grill with flames and steam; tuk-tuks pull into the rank, load passengers and drive off; a skytrain glides over the viaduct and stops at the station; a long-tail boat putters along the canal under the bridge. Everything gives way so nothing ever overlaps.

INTERACTION: drag to pan, right-drag to rotate, scroll or pinch to zoom. Click any stall, shopper, tuk-tuk, train or boat: the camera glides to it and follows if it moves, a ring and label mark it, and a glass panel shows live stats. An area switcher flies between Food Street, Fashion Lane, Canal, Tuk-tuk Rank and Skytrain.

DASHBOARD: frosted glass panels: top bar (logo, search, area switcher, weather menu, live clock, alerts, day/night toggle, user), 3 KPI cards (Shoppers now, Average queue time, Sales tonight), a right detail panel, a bottom-left order tracker (Ordered, Paid, Grilling, Ready, Collected, Rated) and a tabbed table (Stalls, Tuk-tuks, Orders). Realistic data: prices in Thai baht, stall numbers, queue minutes.

EXTRAS: seasons (Clear night, Monsoon downpour with umbrellas popping open and puddles, Songkran with water splashes, Loy Krathong with floating lanterns and krathongs on the canal); animated day/night with glowing string lights; works on mobile.
