Build a single-file interactive web app (HTML, CSS and JS) using Three.js from a CDN: a live 3D emergency command dashboard called "Siren" that feels like a city strategy game.

WORLD: a low-poly isometric city built entirely in code, no external models. A downtown grid with towers, a fire station with bay doors, a police station, a hospital, a river with a bridge, a highway, parks and residential blocks. Clean toy-like clay look, one accent colour (#EF4444 alert red) on cool grey.

LIFE: an incident pin appears (a small building fire with smoke, a traffic collision, a medical call). The nearest unit is dispatched: bay doors open, a fire truck drives out with lights, traffic pulls aside, crews arrive and the smoke fades as the fire is put out. Police cars patrol. Many incidents resolve in parallel. Vehicles never overlap.

INTERACTION: drag to pan, right-drag to rotate, scroll or pinch to zoom. Click any incident, unit or station: the camera glides to it and follows a moving unit, a ring and label mark it, and a glass panel shows live stats. An area switcher flies between Downtown, Riverside, Highway and Residential.

DASHBOARD: frosted glass panels: top bar (logo, search, area switcher, weather menu, live clock, alerts, day/night toggle, user), 3 KPI cards (Active incidents, Average response time, Units available), a right detail panel, a bottom-left incident tracker (Reported, Dispatched, En route, On scene, Contained, Closed) and a tabbed table (Incidents, Units, Stations). Use fictional incident IDs only.

EXTRAS: weather (Clear, Monsoon flooding with water rising on low roads, Fog, Heatwave), animated day/night with flashing emergency lights; works on mobile.
